# 2026-09-21

- Defined the balanced production security posture for TechSupport and ordinary users: keyless
  public relay, cold encrypted root, short-lived delegates for routine support, automatic browser
  v3/Android Keystore custody for normal users, and honest identity-loss behavior.
- Documented that the current TechSupport agent's root-key `localStorage` injection is an interim
  risk boundary. It must not run on the VPS or persist against remotely served code; a local
  signer/decrypter boundary is the next hardening step before TechSupport becomes high-trust.
- Enterprise controls such as HSMs, threshold approval, and formal audits are deferred until scale,
  regulation, staffing, or threat level justifies them.
