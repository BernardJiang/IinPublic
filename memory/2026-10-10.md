  range changes reachability, not audience eligibility. Retire Radio nearby as a location/privacy
  mode and keep Bluetooth as an independent provider switch. Multiple provider sightings must merge
  into one verified peer/session. Hub-down cold discovery involving browsers remains a gap.
- Final correction: Nearby must remain a shared, travelable chatroom, not a personalized nearest-user
  list or a parent/child chatroom tree. With at most `C` users, everyone shares one broad group;
  over-capacity groups are replaced by disjoint geographically compact groups, and adjacent sparse
  groups coalesce after hysteresis. A traveler selects a map point and joins the shared group covering
  it. Exact nearest-498 membership is incompatible with a common roster, so the promise is a
  locality-optimized shared room capped by experimental global `C=498`. All providers require the
  same active-room capability and merge sightings.
- Location privacy settings now include Location off, Regional (~50 km), Citywide (~10 km),
  Neighborhood (~1 km), and Close (~400 m). Location off stops automatic Nearby while Contacts and
  deliberately selected Places remain usable. Larger blur reduces precision but is not an anonymity
  guarantee. Saved Radio-nearby audience selections migrate to Neighborhood.
- Chatrooms UI is flat in both product state and naming: **Your current group** plus flat **Places**;
  the List/Map toggle no longer carries tree IDs/state. Map empty-space taps preview Nearby at that
