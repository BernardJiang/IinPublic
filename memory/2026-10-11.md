  serialized reservations/commits, deterministic reconciliation, hard-cap enforcement, and REST

  a pending reservation could trigger a split that invalidated another live token.

  physical experiment. Capacity 498 remains experimental, not empirically validated.

  automatic parent-to-child moves were incorrectly encoded as manual same-level visits. Added
  exact-status upstream cohort forwarding and corrected the proof choice, with regression tests.

  bidirectional Talks and post-background delivery (p50 18.811 s / p95 19.207 s). The available
  Android 7 Honor reports Wi-Fi Direct hardware but cannot use the app-chosen credential join that
