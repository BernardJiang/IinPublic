  Direct Messages. The message picker remains usable without selecting a person first, and the
  Broadcast button now uses the same neutral styling as the other actions.

  reappear on a deliberate return to Map view but live room/location refreshes do not keep them
  visible. Mandatory map attribution remains visible.
- OPEN-38 current-build hardware gate: PH-1 sender to sleeping P30 passed after a five-minute
  screen-off interval; generic native activity notification appeared 22 seconds after broadcast,
  and the Talk arrived after wake. C10 Android 14 failed: its vendor power controller force-stops
  the entire IinPublic process roughly 90 seconds after screen-off, killing the foreground service,
  embedded node, alarms, and long-poll. An in-process fix cannot notify in that state; a
  content-free OS-managed push (when a hub is reachable) is the remaining option, while offline
  delivery must wait for reopen.
- Native E2E bootstrap must dismiss the first-run encounter-location picker before the walkthrough;
  the picker is appended later and its map canvas otherwise intercepts the walkthrough close click.
