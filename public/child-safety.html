IinPublic Child Safety Standards

Effective September 29, 2026

IinPublic has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

How IinPublic works

IinPublic is peer-to-peer. Talks and messages are exchanged directly between members' devices, and we have no ability to view, scan, or moderate that content — there is no central server holding a readable copy for us to review. There are no accounts, no passwords, and nothing for us to delete on a user's behalf. Age is self-declared and optionally vouched for by other members; we do not independently verify it, and a user can decline it entirely.

If you encounter CSAE

Because we cannot see or monitor content moving between members, and cannot guarantee timely monitoring of any inbox we might offer, the effective way to report child sexual abuse content or behavior is directly to the people equipped to act on it:

  • NCMEC CyberTipline (US): report.cybertip.org or 1-800-843-5678
  • Your local police, especially if a child may be in immediate danger
  • Your country's equivalent reporting authority, if outside the US

Our role

If we become directly aware of apparent CSAM — including through a report sent to us — we will report it to NCMEC and cooperate with law enforcement, as required by applicable law (including 18 U.S.C. §2258A in the US). We do not operate a monitored inbox for CSAE reports, and users and third parties should not rely on contacting us as the way to report a concern — go directly to the authorities above.
