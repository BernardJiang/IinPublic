IinPublic Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

The short version: IinPublic is a decentralized, peer-to-peer app. There is no sign-up with an email or password, and no central account database of your conversations, profile answers, or location. The name is the design: you decide what's in public, one answer at a time, and you can change your mind later. The one exception is messages you send to TechSupport, our built-in support account — see below. IinPublic is also open source (MIT license), so you don't have to take our word for any of this — see below.

No account, no email, no password

Creating an identity on IinPublic does not require an email address, phone number, or password. Your identity is a cryptographic key pair generated and kept on your own device. We never see or store it, and we cannot reset or recover it for you if it's lost — that's the trade-off of not holding an account database in the first place.

Talks, profile answers, and chat messages

Talks (the questions you send or answer) and post-match chat messages are exchanged directly between members' devices over an encrypted peer-to-peer connection, and stored on those devices — not in a central database we operate. Our relay servers help devices discover and reach each other, but are designed not to retain a readable copy of that content.

Your profile Q&A is only shared with the visibility you choose for each answer (public, contacts-only, or private). Nothing you haven't answered, and nothing you've marked private, is shared at all.

Changing your mind about a public answer

If you answered a question publicly and later regret it, you can:

  • Switch it from auto back to manual, so your chatbot stops repeating it to people who ask in the future, or
  • Remove the question/answer from your profile entirely.

Either way, this takes effect immediately for anything shared from that point forward. Because IinPublic is peer-to-peer rather than a central service, this cannot reach back and erase a copy that another member's device already received before you made the change — there is no central database for us to delete it from on your behalf. Think of it like anything you've said in public: you can stop saying it, but you can't unsay it to people who already heard it.

Location

If you allow location access, it's used to place you in a regional chat room and to support distance-based matching. Your device blurs your precise coordinates to a coarse (roughly 2km × 2km) area before anything is shared with the network — we only ever receive and store that coarse area, never your exact location. Location access is optional; declining it just limits region-based features.

Camera

Camera access is used only when you choose to take a profile photo, or scan a QR code to link a second device to your existing identity. We don't access the camera at any other time, and photos aren't analyzed or shared beyond what you explicitly add to your profile.

Large files, photos, and videos

Beyond a profile photo, you can also attach larger files, photos, and videos to a talk or conversation. These are sent over IPFS, a peer-to-peer file network, directly to the people you share them with — the same peer-to-peer design as your talks and messages. We don't collect or store copies of these attachments on our servers.

Financial transactions

IinPublic never supports payments, money transfers, or any other financial transaction — there is no feature for it anywhere in the app. As a safety measure, the app automatically detects and blocks anything that looks like a payment card number, bank account/routing number, or similar financial detail before it can be sent to another member, whether or not you meant to send it. Never rely on this app to exchange money.

Nearby devices (Bluetooth / Wi-Fi)

On Android, IinPublic can request Bluetooth and Wi-Fi nearby-device permissions to discover other IinPublic devices nearby for direct, local peer-to-peer message delivery — for example, when the internet is unavailable. These permissions are declared to Android as never used for location, and we do not use them to determine or track where you are.

Age verification

Some content is age-gated (for example, questions about dating or age range). Age verification on IinPublic is peer-based: other members confirm your age through the app, rather than you submitting a government ID or other document to us. We never collect a copy of any ID.

Blocking

Your block list is encrypted and stored under your own key. We cannot read who you've blocked.

TechSupport

TechSupport is the one built-in account operated by us, for in-app help. It may be answered by more than one person on our side, not a single individual. Unlike ordinary talks and messages, conversations you have with TechSupport are handled by our own server-backed system so we (the people running TechSupport) can see and respond to them. Don't send anything to TechSupport you wouldn't want us to read.

Deleting your data

Because your content lives on your own device (and, once shared, on the devices of the people you shared it with) rather than in a central database, uninstalling the app or clearing its site data removes your local copy. There is no "delete my account" request to send us, because there is no account of your content for us to hold.

Children

IinPublic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Open source

IinPublic's source code is public and published under the MIT license at github.com/BernardJiang/IinPublic — anyone can read exactly how data is handled rather than relying solely on this description of it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and post the revised version at this same address.

Contact
