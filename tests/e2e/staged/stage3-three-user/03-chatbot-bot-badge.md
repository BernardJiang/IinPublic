# Test: Chatbot — Bot Badge Visibility

covers: SPEC-12.3  <!-- auto-seeded; refine by hand -->

**Features tested:** Chatbot auto-reply mode, bot badge display on matched conversations

---

## What this test does (in plain English):

Three users: Tom, Jerry, and Bob, all in the "Global" chatroom.

1. **Tom creates a talk and broadcasts it**
2. **Jerry enables the chatbot** (an automated reply feature) and answers the talk
3. **The chatbot saves Jerry's answer** as a template for future talks
4. **Using `announceTalkToRoom`**, the system announces the talk again
5. **Bob's conversation** with Jerry shows a **bot badge** (indicating Jerry's response was auto-generated by the chatbot, not manually typed)
6. **Tom's conversation** does NOT show a bot badge (Tom responded normally)

## Verifications:

- ✅ When a user has the chatbot enabled, their auto-generated answers are marked with a bot badge
- ✅ Manual conversations don't show the bot badge
- ✅ The bot badge helps distinguish real-time human replies from automated chatbot replies
