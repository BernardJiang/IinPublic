# Phone path 04 — mismatch, block and leave across two phones

Alice is on phone A and Bob on phone B, in the same Nearby room.

1. **Mismatch** (J-TALK-04): Alice broadcasts a flow Talk and Bob picks the non-matching answer.
   Bob's Answers list shows it as a mismatch; neither status bar shows a match, and neither has a
   conversation with the other.
2. **Block** (J-TALK-05): Bob gets Alice's first Talk; he blocks her from her profile
   (⋯ → Block User); the hub records the block; Alice's next Talk never reaches Bob's phone.
3. **Leave** (J-ROOM-03): Alice moves into a Place of her own — Bob's member list and headcount
   drop her and so does the hub; she comes back and Bob sees her again; she closes the app and Bob's
   list, headcount and the hub drop her again.
