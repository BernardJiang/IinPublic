## Daily routine (2–3 minutes)

1. Open each swap app for 20–30 seconds.
2. Check our AndroidClosedTesting thread and Reddit posts for new swap offers; for each one:
   join their group, opt in as iinpublic2026, install, reply "Done, opted in".
3. Now and then: check our group's Members page and the Play Console tester count.

Feedback for each swap app (once, around day 3–7): two or three honest sentences on its Play
Store page (testing apps show a feedback box) or in their thread. Language barriers and paywalls
are valid feedback.

## Test steps we give our testers

Pinned in the tester group (original post plus two updates) and in the group's welcome message,
which is emailed to new members:

- **Day 1 (about 5 minutes):** read the welcome tour; Settings tab → Profile: replace the random
  user name with a stage name (no account needed); Talks tab: tap the tags you like, ignore the
  rest; (optional) Chatrooms tab: check the status of communication with others; Contacts tab:
  see your top 3 most similar people.
- **Every day (1 minute):** Contacts tab — has the top 3 changed?
- **Optional:** A) Talks tab → "create my tag" (a keyword, phrase or short sentence), see whether
  others like it; B) create a Talk with a few multiple-choice questions; C) near another tester,
  check whether tags arrive over Wi-Fi / Wi-Fi Direct without mobile data.
- **Feedback before day 14:** reply to the pinned post (Groups hides email addresses in posts).

## When we reach 12

After 12 testers have stayed opted in for 14 days: Play Console → Dashboard → **Apply for
production**. Google asks how testers were recruited, what they did and what feedback came in —
this file and the replies in the pinned thread are the answers.

## Gotchas

- Closed-test apps don't show up in Play search; open the opt-in or store link directly.
- In Chrome, Google Groups for iinpublic2026 is `/u/1/`, and Play is `?authuser=1`.
- Google Groups can't edit a posted message; post an update as a reply in the same thread so
  links stay valid.
- Twelve Testers and Reddit: Claude can't post on Reddit (blocked for its browser tools); it can
  fill in Twelve Testers once you're logged in there.
