# Two-phone offline Wi-Fi Direct check

Use this when two Android phones near each other do not appear in IinPublic after airplane mode is enabled. Do not clear app data or reinstall just to retry: each installation holds its own identity and history.

1. On **both** phones, open IinPublic and enter the **same active Nearby/chatroom**. In **Settings → Nearby exchange**, select **While open** while testing with both apps visible (or **Always** if testing screen-off), and leave **Wi-Fi Direct** enabled. Both phones must run IinPublic; Android's generic Wi-Fi Direct device list is not the app's verified Nearby list.
2. Turn on airplane mode, then turn **Wi-Fi** and **Bluetooth** back on in Android quick settings. Keep both phones close and their screens awake for the first check. On Android 10–12, also turn **Location** on; Wi-Fi Direct discovery on those versions requires it even without internet.
3. Check **Android Settings → Apps → IinPublic → Permissions** on each phone. Allow **Nearby devices** (Wi-Fi and Bluetooth) on Android 13+, or **Precise/Fine location** on Android 10–12. A granted system radio switch does not grant the app permission. Return to IinPublic after changing a permission.
4. Read IinPublic's **Settings → Nearby exchange** status on each phone. Record whether it says **Searching**, **checking again**, **hosting**, **joined**, or names a blocked permission/radio. The offline scan opens for about 30 seconds and backs off when nobody is found; allow a few minutes, or toggle the app's Wi-Fi Direct switch off and back on to retry immediately. Do not infer a connection from Wi-Fi being enabled alone.
5. If both still show Searching, record each phone model, IinPublic version, exact active room, Nearby mode, Wi-Fi Direct/Bluetooth switch states, Android Wi-Fi/Bluetooth/Location and app-permission states, the approximate wait time, and whether either phone ever showed **hosting** or **joined**. Keep the apps installed so diagnostics can be collected when the phones reconnect over USB.

On the 2026-10-29 USB check, C10 (Android 14) had **Nearby Wi-Fi Devices and Bluetooth permissions denied**, P30 (Android 10) had Fine Location granted and Wi-Fi/Location on, and Honor FCP-AN10 (Android 16) had Nearby permissions granted but Wi-Fi was off **at the time of the check**. These states were observed after the park visit and do not prove what each switch showed at the park. The affected phone pair is still to be confirmed.
